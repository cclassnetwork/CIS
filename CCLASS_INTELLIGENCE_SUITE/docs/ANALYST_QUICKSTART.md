# Analyst quick-start

1. **Sign in.** Ask your administrator for an account. Turn on two-factor authentication from the user menu.
2. **Create a case** (Investigations → New): name, priority, **TLP** marking, tags, due date, assignee.
3. **Investigate.** Search an email, domain, IP, username, place or CVE on the Search page, or run a single module
   from Modules. Use `GLOBALWATCH` with `Country`, `Country | State` or `global` for news watch.
4. **Review findings** by severity and confidence. Confidence reflects source reliability (A–F), data quality and age.
   Sample/offline data is always low.
5. **Add evidence** (Evidence tab). Files are hashed with SHA-256 and metadata is extracted.
6. **Work together** in the Discussion tab; assign the case and set a due date. "Mine" and "Overdue" filters help.
7. **Report** (Reports): HTML, PDF, CSV, JSON or TXT. The report carries the case's TLP marking.

## TLP markings

| Marking | Share with |
|---|---|
| TLP:RED | Named recipients only |
| TLP:AMBER+STRICT | Your organisation only |
| TLP:AMBER | Your organisation and those who need to know |
| TLP:GREEN | Your community |
| TLP:CLEAR | Anyone |

## Rules of use

Public-source and authorised data only. Network inventory only on networks you own or have written permission to
scan. No private communications, no access to accounts that are not yours, no covert tracking of individuals.
Every action you take is recorded in the audit log.
